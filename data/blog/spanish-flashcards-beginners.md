If you're learning Spanish as a beginner, the question isn't "which cards should I make?" — it's "which 100 give me the highest return per minute?" This guide curates the first 100 Spanish flashcards every beginner needs, organized by theme and frequency.

## Selection Criteria

Three filters narrowed thousands of words to 100:

1. **Top 1000 most-used Spanish words** (frequency)
2. **Daily-life relevance** (will you use this word weekly?)
3. **Beginner accessibility** (avoid technical or formal vocabulary)

## How to Learn These 100 Cards

### Week 1: Tier 1A + 1B (30 cards)
Survival + family. 15-20 minutes daily.

### Week 2: Tier 1C + 1D (30 cards)
Numbers + verbs. Add daily routine practice.

### Week 3: Tier 1E + 1F (20 cards)
Food + places. Connect to real-world application.

### Week 4: Tier 1G + 1H + 1I (20 cards)
Body + colors + adjectives. Round out beginner vocabulary.

By Week 5, you have 100 high-frequency Spanish words.

## Implementation Tips

- **Use both sides:** Spanish on one side, English + picture on other
- **Distributed practice:** 15 min daily beats 2 hours on Saturday
- **Speak aloud:** pronunciation matters; listen to native audio
- **Apply immediately:** use new words in conversation or writing
- **Review old cards:** previously learned words need 5-7 reviews to stick

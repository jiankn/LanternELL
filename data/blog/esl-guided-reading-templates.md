Guided reading is the highest-leverage instructional moment in ESL — small group, level-aligned, intensive. But guided reading without templates becomes improvised. This guide gives you 5 ESL guided reading templates that structure the 5-day cycle from pre-reading to assessment.

For our complete library, see [ESL reading worksheets](/esl-reading-worksheets). For broader strategies, read [ESL reading comprehension strategies](/teaching-tips/esl-reading-comprehension-strategies).

## The 5-Day ESL Guided Reading Cycle

### Day 1: Picture Walk + Vocabulary Preview
- **Template:** Pre-reading vocabulary preview worksheet
- **Activity:** Walk through book illustrations. Predict story. Preview 5-7 key words.
- **Time:** 20 min

### Day 2: First Read + Echo Reading
- **Template:** During-reading sticky note prompts
- **Activity:** Teacher echo-reads. Students mark unfamiliar words with sticky notes.
- **Time:** 20 min

### Day 3: Comprehension Questions
- **Template:** Comprehension question worksheet (4 question types: Right There / Think and Search / On My Own / Author and Me)
- **Activity:** Students answer 4-6 questions. Discuss in group.
- **Time:** 20 min

### Day 4: Story Mapping + Retell
- **Template:** Story map graphic organizer
- **Activity:** Students fill in characters, setting, problem, solution. Retell to partner.
- **Time:** 20 min

### Day 5: Writing Connection
- **Template:** Connection journal (text-to-self, text-to-text, text-to-world)
- **Activity:** Students write a 3-sentence connection.
- **Time:** 20 min

## Template 2: During-Reading Sticky Note Prompts

Three sticky note prompts to use during reading:

1. ❓ **Question:** What does this word/sentence mean?
2. ⭐ **Important:** This is a key idea
3. 🔗 **Connection:** This reminds me of...

Students use the corresponding sticky note while reading.

## Differentiating Templates by WIDA Level

### WIDA Level 1 (Newcomer)
- **Day 1:** Picture walk only, vocabulary in home language
- **Day 2:** Echo reading, no sticky notes
- **Day 3:** Right There questions only, picture answers OK
- **Day 4:** Story map with drawings
- **Day 5:** Skip writing, do retell instead

### WIDA Level 2 (Beginning)
- All templates standard
- Allow drawings + 1-3 word written responses
- Bilingual sentence frames

### WIDA Level 3+ (Developing)
- All templates standard
- Full sentence written responses
- All 4 QAR question types

## Group Composition Tips

- **Group size:** 4-6 students max
- **WIDA range:** Spread of 1 level only (e.g., all Level 2-3, not Level 1-4)
- **Time:** 20 min per group, 4 rotations per day
- **Books:** Same book per group, leveled to group

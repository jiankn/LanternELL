## Tier 1: First-Week Survival (10 worksheets)

These are the **absolute first 10 to print** when you get a newcomer in your K classroom. Without these, the student can&apos;t navigate basic routines.

### Day 1 Pack (4 worksheets)
1. **Name & Country card** — bilingual, student fills in name, age, country, language
2. **Greetings frames** — Hello / Hola, Goodbye / Adiós, with picture cues
3. **Classroom objects (8-card mini)** — pencil, book, chair, desk, paper, scissors, glue, eraser
4. **Bathroom + water request frames** — picture-only worksheet for survival communication

### Day 2-3 Pack (3 worksheets)
5. **Daily routine sequencing** — morning meeting → snack → lunch → recess → dismissal
6. **Feelings faces matching** — happy, sad, tired, scared, excited (so student can communicate non-verbally)
7. **Numbers 1-5 tracing** — minimal, picture-supported

### Day 4-5 Pack (3 worksheets)
8. **Colors tracing (red, blue, yellow, green)** — first 4 colors only
9. **Family members coloring** — mom, dad, brother, sister with stick-figure templates
10. **Five-page mini-book combining colors + numbers** — first reading practice

## Tier 2: Daily Routine Vocabulary (10 worksheets)

After Week 1 stabilizes, expand vocabulary across daily routines.

### Body & Self-Care (3 worksheets)
11. **Body parts diagram labeling** — head, hands, feet, eyes, mouth, tummy
12. **Five senses chart** — see, hear, smell, taste, touch
13. **Healthy habits sorting** — wash hands, brush teeth, eat fruit, drink water

### Food & Lunch (3 worksheets)
14. **Lunch tray vocabulary** — fork, spoon, plate, napkin, milk, water
15. **Food groups sorting** — fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy
16. **My favorite food** — picture frame with sentence "I like ___."

### School & Centers (4 worksheets)
17. **School supplies labeling** — expanded from Day 1 (now adds notebook, ruler, marker, crayon)
18. **Classroom areas labeling** — reading corner, math center, art table
19. **Center direction cards** — picture-supported, "go to ___ first"
20. **Carpet meeting picture cues** — sit, listen, raise hand, share

## Tier 3: Theme & Content Vocabulary (10 worksheets)

Once daily routines are stable, build content-area vocabulary.

### Animals (2 worksheets)
21. **Farm animals sorting** — cow, pig, chicken, horse, sheep
22. **Pet vs farm vs zoo** — sort 12 animals into 3 categories

### Weather & Seasons (2 worksheets)
23. **Weather wheel** — sunny, cloudy, rainy, snowy, windy
24. **Four seasons labeling** — spring, summer, fall, winter with picture clues

### Shapes & Numbers (3 worksheets)
25. **Shapes in real life** — find a circle, square, triangle in the classroom
26. **Numbers 1-20 fill-in** — count objects, write the numeral
27. **Counting songs worksheet** — "Five Little Monkeys" with picture cues

### Action Verbs (3 worksheets)
28. **TPR action cards** — run, jump, sit, stand, walk, skip, hop
29. **Verb-noun matching** — match action to thing (eat the apple, drink the water)
30. **Action mini-book** — connected text using week&apos;s action verbs

## How to Choose Which to Print First

If you have a brand-new K newcomer arriving Monday, print these 10 in this order before they walk in:

1. Name & Country card (#1)
2. Greetings frames (#2)
3. Classroom objects 8-card mini (#3)
4. Bathroom + water frames (#4)
5. Daily routine sequencing (#5)
6. Feelings faces (#6)
7. Numbers 1-5 tracing (#7)
8. Colors tracing (#8)
9. Family coloring (#9)
10. Mini-book (#10)

That gets you through Week 1. Then expand into Tier 2 in Week 2, and Tier 3 in Weeks 3-4.

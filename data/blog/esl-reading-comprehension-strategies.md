Reading comprehension is the highest-stakes skill in ESL instruction. Without it, content access is impossible. This guide curates 8 research-backed strategies that work for ELL students at WIDA Levels 1-5, with specific implementation steps for each.

## Strategy 1: Picture Walk (Pre-Reading)

### What It Is
Before reading, walk through illustrations only. Students predict story + preview vocabulary.

### Why It Works
ELL students often can&apos;t access vocabulary through text alone. Pictures provide universal entry points.

### Implementation
1. Open the book or text
2. Show only illustrations (cover up text)
3. Ask: "What do you see?" "What do you think will happen?"
4. List vocabulary that appears in illustrations
5. Then read the text

### Time: 5-7 minutes before reading

## Strategy 2: Echo Reading (During Reading)

### What It Is
Teacher reads, students repeat exactly.

### Why It Works
Builds pronunciation, prosody, and fluency simultaneously. Reduces affective filter.

### Implementation
1. Choose a 50-100 word passage
2. Read first sentence aloud with strong expression
3. Students echo (repeat exactly)
4. Continue line-by-line
5. Re-read the full passage together once

### Time: 8-10 minutes per session

## Strategy 3: Cloze Deletion (During Reading)

### What It Is
Remove 1 word in 7. Students predict from context.

### Why It Works
Forces students to use surrounding words and pictures to infer meaning — the core comprehension skill.

### Implementation
1. Choose a passage students can mostly read
2. White out every 7th word (or use cloze worksheet)
3. Students fill in based on context
4. Discuss what clues helped

### Time: 12-15 minutes

## Strategy 4: Story Mapping (Post-Reading)

### What It Is
Graphic organizer with characters, setting, problem, solution.

### Why It Works
Externalizes story structure. ELL students who can't articulate "what happened" can still draw the map.

### Implementation
1. Provide blank story map worksheet
2. Students fill in (drawings or words OK)
3. Pair share — explain map to a partner
4. Whole-class debrief

### Time: 15 minutes after reading

## Strategy 5: Retell with Pictures (Post-Reading)

### What It Is
Sequence picture cards retelling the story.

### Why It Works
Bypasses verbal limitations. Newcomers can demonstrate comprehension without speaking.

### Implementation
1. Pre-make 6-8 picture cards from the story (or use ones included with worksheet)
2. Mix the cards
3. Students sequence them
4. Optional: add captions in either language

### Time: 10 minutes

## Strategy 6: Question-Answer Relationships (QAR)

### What It Is
Teach students 4 question types: Right There / Think and Search / On My Own / Author and Me.

### Why It Works
ELL students often don't know which questions can be found in text vs require inference. QAR makes this explicit.

### Implementation
1. Introduce one question type at a time (start with Right There)
2. Practice with multiple texts
3. Add Think and Search after 2 weeks
4. Add On My Own and Author and Me after another 2 weeks

### Time: 5-10 minutes per question type

## Strategy 7: Vocabulary in Context (Pre-Reading)

### What It Is
Pre-teach 5-7 key vocabulary words BEFORE reading, with picture support and example sentences.

### Why It Works
Reduces cognitive load during reading. Students focus on comprehension, not decoding.

### Implementation
1. Identify 5-7 key vocabulary from the text
2. Display vocabulary cards (bilingual when possible)
3. Provide picture support and example sentence for each
4. Students chant or trace each word
5. Then read the text

### Time: 10 minutes pre-reading

## Strategy 8: Reciprocal Teaching (Advanced)

### What It Is
Students take turns being teacher: predicting, questioning, clarifying, summarizing.

### Why It Works
Distributes comprehension monitoring. Students develop metacognitive skills.

### Implementation
1. Model each role (predict, question, clarify, summarize) with whole class
2. Form small groups (3-4 students)
3. One student takes each role per paragraph
4. Rotate roles every paragraph

### Time: 20 minutes; best for WIDA Level 4-5

## Strategy Selection by WIDA Level

| WIDA Level | Best Strategies |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Level 1 (Newcomer) | Picture Walk, Vocabulary in Context |
| Level 2 (Beginning) | Echo Reading, Retell with Pictures |
| Level 3 (Developing) | Cloze Deletion, Story Mapping |
| Level 4-5 (Expanding) | QAR, Reciprocal Teaching |

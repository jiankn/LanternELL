## What to Look for in Free ESL Reading PDFs

Quality criteria:

- **Picture support** for K-3 (or vocabulary preview for 4-8)
- **Comprehension questions** included (not just the passage)
- **WIDA alignment** noted (or at minimum, level-appropriate)
- **Bilingual option** for newcomer students
- **Answer key** for self-checking

Most free PDFs miss 2-3 of these. Curate accordingly.

## Common Pitfalls in Free PDFs

### Pitfall 1: No Comprehension Questions
Many free PDFs are just the passage with no questions. You build the assessment yourself. Time-consuming.

### Pitfall 2: No WIDA Alignment
Free sites rarely note WIDA levels. You guess based on text complexity.

### Pitfall 3: Watermarked
Some free PDFs have site logos on every page. Fine for emergency use, ugly for portfolio.

### Pitfall 4: One-Off, No Series
Free PDFs are usually single passages. Hard to build a multi-week unit.

### Pitfall 5: No Bilingual Option
Free reading PDFs are almost universally English-only. For Level 1-2 ELLs, this is a major gap.

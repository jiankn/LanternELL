If you Google "vocabulary worksheets" you get 50 million results, 80% of them useless. This list is different: it's 50 vocabulary worksheets that K-5 teachers actually use, organized by theme and grade band, with notes on which ones work for ELL students.

For a complete overview, see our main [vocabulary worksheets](/vocabulary-worksheets) page. For grade-level planning, read [vocabulary worksheets by grade level](/teaching-tips/vocabulary-worksheets-by-grade-level).

## K-2: Foundation Vocabulary (Tier 1)

K-2 vocabulary instruction focuses on high-frequency words students need every day. These 20 worksheets cover the foundations.

### Classroom & School (5 worksheets)
1. **Classroom objects matching** — pair pencil, book, chair, desk with pictures
2. **School supplies fill-in** — complete sentences like "I need a ___."
3. **Classroom areas labeling** — reading corner, math center, art table
4. **Daily routines sequencing** — morning meeting, lunch, recess, dismissal
5. **Classroom rules vocabulary** — share, listen, raise hand, line up

### Family & Home (4 worksheets)
6. **Family members tree** — mom, dad, brother, sister, grandma, grandpa
7. **Home rooms labeling** — kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room
8. **Family verbs** — cook, eat, sleep, play
9. **My family poster** — bilingual template with photos

### Food & Body (5 worksheets)
10. **Food groups sorting** — fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy
11. **Healthy vs sometimes foods** — categorize 20 common foods
12. **Body parts labeling** — head, shoulders, knees, toes diagram
13. **Five senses chart** — see, hear, smell, taste, touch
14. **Feelings faces matching** — happy, sad, tired, excited, scared, angry

### Colors, Numbers, Shapes (6 worksheets)
15. **Color words tracing** — 8 basic colors
16. **Numbers 1-20 word + numeral matching**
17. **Counting with objects** — pictures + number words
18. **Shapes identification** — circle, square, triangle, rectangle, oval
19. **Shapes in real life** — find the shape in everyday objects
20. **Opposites matching** — big/small, hot/cold, up/down

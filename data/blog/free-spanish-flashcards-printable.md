## What to Look for in Free Spanish Flashcards

Quality criteria:

- **Native-Spanish reviewed** (not Google Translate)
- **Clear illustrations** (not stock clip art)
- **Pronunciation guide** included
- **Print-ready PDF** (not screenshot images)
- **Cardstock-friendly** (right size for laminating)

Most free sites miss 2-3 of these. This guide filters out the weak ones.

- **Cultural diversity** — most show only Mexican-American context

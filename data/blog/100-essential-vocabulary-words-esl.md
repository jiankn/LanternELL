If you teach ESL students at WIDA Levels 1-3, the question isn't "which vocabulary should I teach?" — it's "which 100 words give my students the highest return per minute of instruction?" This list answers that question with 100 vocabulary words tested in real ELL classrooms across K-8.

## How These 100 Were Chosen

Three criteria filtered tens of thousands of candidate words:

1. **Frequency:** Top 1000 most-used English words (Davies Corpus)
2. **Classroom relevance:** Words students need to navigate K-8 schooling
3. **Survival impact:** Words that prevent communication breakdown for newcomers

The result: 100 words that appear in nearly every K-8 ESL classroom every day.

## Implementation Plan

### Days 1-5: Tier 1A (20 words)
Use bilingual flashcards. Quick chants. Survival worksheets. By Friday, the student can ask for the bathroom + water.

### Weeks 2-4: Tier 1B (25 words)
Centers + routines. Daily integration during morning meeting. Add 5-7 words per week.

### Weeks 5-8: Tier 1C-D (30 words)
Family + body themes. Pair with picture books. Send-home practice for parent involvement.

### Weeks 9-12: Tier 1E-F (25 words)
Numbers, colors, food. Connects to math + P.E. + lunch routines.

By the end of the first quarter, your ESL student has 100 words — enough to engage in basic K-2 instruction.

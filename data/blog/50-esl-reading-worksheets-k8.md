## How to Use This List

### Week 1-2: Pick 3 worksheets for your level
Don't try all 50. Start with 3 that match your students' WIDA level.

### Weeks 3-8: Build a routine
Use the same 3 worksheets each week with new content. Students learn the format and can focus on content.

### Weeks 9+: Expand vocabulary, not format
Same worksheet types, broader themes. Students don't relearn the worksheet — they apply what they know.

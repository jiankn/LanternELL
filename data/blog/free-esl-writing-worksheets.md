Free ESL writing worksheets are easy to find but hard to evaluate. Most are either too generic to use or too thin to teach with. This guide curates **truly useful free ESL writing worksheets**, organized by grade band and WIDA level.

For our complete library, see [ESL writing worksheets](/esl-writing-worksheets). For 30 prompts by level, read [ESL writing prompts by level](/teaching-tips/esl-writing-prompts-by-level).

## Quality Criteria for Free ESL Writing Worksheets

- **Sentence frames included** for Level 1-3
- **Picture prompts** for visual entry
- **WIDA-level differentiation** noted
- **Answer/example included** for self-assessment
- **Bilingual scaffolds** for newcomers

Most free sites miss 2-3 of these criteria. Use this guide to filter.

## What Free Doesn't Cover

- **WIDA alignment** rare in free
- **Bilingual scaffolds** mostly missing
- **Differentiated rubrics** absent
- **Series organization** — free is one-off
- **Answer keys** rarely included

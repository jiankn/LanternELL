Dual language (DL) classrooms have unique materials needs that English-only and ELL-only classrooms don't. The single biggest mistake new DL teachers make is buying English-Spanish (ELL-focused) materials and trying to retrofit them. This guide explains why Spanish-English worksheets are different, and how to use them in 50/50 or 90/10 DL models.

For our Spanish-English library, see [Spanish-English worksheets](/spanish-english-worksheets). For broader DL curriculum, browse [dual language classroom](/dual-language-classroom) resources.

## What Makes a Worksheet "Dual Language" vs ELL?

DL worksheets serve two student groups simultaneously: native English speakers learning Spanish, and native Spanish speakers learning English. Both directions equal. ELL worksheets serve one direction (Spanish → English).

Three structural differences:

1. **Language order:** "Spanish-English" or alternating, not "English with Spanish caption"
2. **Tasks bidirectional:** Same task works regardless of student's home language
3. **Cultural neutrality:** Content draws equally from English-speaking and Spanish-speaking contexts

## The 50/50 vs 90/10 Models

### 50/50 Model
Half the day in Spanish, half in English. Students need:
- Spanish vocabulary worksheets for Spanish content blocks
- English vocabulary worksheets for English content blocks
- Or **bidirectional Spanish-English worksheets** that work both ways

### 90/10 Model
90% Spanish in K-1, gradually shifting to 50/50 by 4th-5th grade. Students need:
- Spanish-dominant materials in early grades
- Spanish-English bridging materials starting 2nd grade
- Bidirectional materials throughout

## Daily Use in DL Classrooms

### Vocabulary Block (15 min)
- Whole-group introduction in target language of the day
- Picture-supported vocabulary cards (bilingual)
- Choral repetition

### Practice Center (20 min)
- Matching, tracing, fill-in-blank in target language
- Bidirectional materials let mixed-proficiency pairs work together

### Mini-Book Reading (15 min)
- Read in the day's target language
- Comprehension check via picture-pointing

### Application (15 min)
- Sentence frames + writing
- Spanish-English worksheets provide frames in both languages

## Differentiating Within DL

### Native Spanish Speakers Learning English
- Use English-side first
- Spanish provides comprehension scaffold
- Goal: English production by week 3

### Native English Speakers Learning Spanish
- Use Spanish-side first
- English provides comprehension scaffold
- Goal: Spanish production by week 3

### True Balanced Bilinguals
- Alternate sides daily
- Don't privilege one language
